## Game Jams
All of the following were made with Unity, unless otherwise stated.
### Lily Frog (GMTK Game Jam 2025)
This game was made with friends from university: Jeremy, David, Andy, and Alvin.

The theme for this jam was *Loop*, so we made a simple platformer-like game where you swing around obstacles. We used a frog because it's cute, and we chose to have the frog (Lily) pollinate flowers on their way to their destination while also avoiding obstacles like birds and thorns. I did much of the game systems programming (e.g., character controller, level win conditions, etc.), though David, Jeremy, and Alvin also did a good deal of programming work. The art was primarily done by David (though Andy did the background art), and Jeremy did the music and selected the sound effects.

[You can play the game here, on itch.io](https://aweq.itch.io/lily-frog)

### Close Enough (GMTK Game Jam 2024)
A project I did with a close friend, David Babazadeh.

The theme for this jam was *Built to Scale*, so we made a game where you have to resize two objects in order to get them to be the correct relative scale. We both shared programming responsibilities, I did the art, and he did the music. I think the game is rather fun and clever for what it is, and I think you could get some good laughs out of it.

[You can play the game here, on itch.io](https://summedup.itch.io/close-enough)

### Spin to Win (Pirate Jam 16)
The theme was *You are the Weapon*, and we made a physics-driven game where you are a beyblade-like top with weapons growing out of you. You need to assemble the best assortment of weapons to beat your opponent. Our game was heavily inspired by autobattlers like *The Bazaar* and *Team Fight Tactics*.

Our gameplay came out a bit underbaked, but it was a worthwhile learning experience for us all. My teammates were Anthony Langer (art and UI) and Oren Dror (music). I did the programming and game engine work for this game.

#### Mazzie's Mission in the Mimic Museum
![mazzies_mimic_museum](/assets/mazzie_attack.png){:class="img-responsive" width="50%"}

The theme for this jam was *Halloween*.

This game is a prop-hunt-inspired game where you clear out a museum of the mimics lying within. The mimics only move toward you if you aren't looking at them. If you break too many real artifacts, you lose. Mazzie has a basic attack ability and an ability to reveal the mimics around her (which takes more mana). I made this game solo. This game used free assets from [here](https://9e0.itch.io/witches-pack) and some other itch.io asset packs.
